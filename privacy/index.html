GOOD TO KNOW · PRIVACY

Your plans.
Your control.

This page describes the current VELORA starter website—not features that may be added later.

Last reviewed: 26 November 2026

1. What this version stores

The current website does not create user accounts or maintain a server-side travel profile. Some features use your browser's local storage on the device where you use VELORA:

  • Saved moments and ideas.
  • Checklist selections.
  • A trip-budget draft, only when you choose to save it.

Planner and style suggestions are generated in the browser. Your destination, date, style choices and budget values are not submitted to VELORA's API by those tools in this build.

2. Local storage and your choices

Local storage remains in the browser profile on the device you used. It is not an account, does not sync across devices and may be deleted when you clear site data, use private browsing or change devices. Avoid saving personal or sensitive details on a shared device. Use the clear/reset controls where available, or clear the site's browser data.

3. Hosting and external requests

VELORA is served through Cloudflare Pages. Like other websites, hosting infrastructure may process technical request information needed to deliver and protect the service, such as IP address, request time, browser and requested resource.

The homepage currently requests font files from Google Fonts. Those requests go to Google domains and are subject to Google's own privacy terms. Links to official sources and third-party providers take you away from VELORA; review the destination site's policies before using them.

4. Analytics, advertising and accounts

No third-party analytics tracker, advertising pixel, newsletter system, user account or payment checkout is configured in this starter release. The product catalogue connection is not currently active. This statement must be reviewed before any of those services are introduced.

5. Future changes

Before adding accounts, analytics, external AI, messaging, booking or payments, VELORA must update this notice with the relevant data, purpose, providers, retention and user controls. This starter notice is operational information, not legal advice or a substitute for a jurisdiction-specific legal review.
