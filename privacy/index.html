01 / INFORMATION YOU ENTER

Most planning happens in your browser.

The current homepage planner, style helper and Trip Budget Planner generate their illustrative results in the browser. The planning forms do not send the destination, dates, traveller choices or entered budget amounts to a VELORA application server.

Browser-local storage

Saved moments, checklist selections, budget drafts and complete experience plans you explicitly save use browser storage on the device where you choose to save them. They are not a VELORA account and do not sync across devices. Browser storage may be deleted when you clear site data, use private browsing or your browser removes stored data.

When you choose “Save full plan on this device” on the Experiences page, the complete illustrative outline text—including the destination, any selected date, traveller count, travel style, stay preference and optional budget amount and currency—is stored only in that browser. You can reopen it under Saved moments & plans or export it as a plain-text file. Remove the saved item or clear browser site data to delete it. The plan is not sent to VELORA’s application server.

When you choose “Continue to budget” from a generated getaway plan, the browser temporarily stores that plan's destination, occasion, trip length, traveller count, selected currency and optional budget ceiling so the budget calculator can prefill those fields on the next page. The handoff is one-time and removed after it is applied. It remains on your device and is not sent to a VELORA application server. You can edit the values in the budget calculator before using them.

After calculating a budget, you may choose either the India-arrival or India-outbound preparation checklist. Only the optional destination, trip length and traveller count are stored for that next step, for up to 30 minutes or until the selected checklist opens. The estimated costs, currency, cost breakdown and budget ceiling are not passed to the checklist. The one-time summary is removed when shown and is never sent to VELORA's server.

Do not enter passport numbers, payment-card details, health records or other sensitive information into the prototype.

Local concierge demonstration

VELORA AI Concierge is a planned future capability and is not currently available on the public website. No external AI provider is connected to the current prototype.

There are no public user profiles, direct messages, stranger matching or community posts in the current prototype.

02 / WEBSITE REQUESTS

Running the website still involves network requests.

Even when a planning form keeps its values in the browser, opening a website sends ordinary requests needed to deliver its pages and assets.

01 / HOSTING

Site delivery

VELORA is served through Cloudflare Pages. The hosting provider may process technical request information needed to deliver and protect the site. This page does not make claims about provider log retention periods.

02 / FONTS

Third-party resources

The current pages request web-font resources from Google Fonts. Your browser may contact Google to retrieve those fonts. This is different from sending your saved trip plan to VELORA.

03 / OFFLINE

Service worker cache

On supported browsers, public pages register VELORA's root-scoped service worker after the page loads. It caches selected static pages and assets for faster repeat visits and limited offline use. The first installation requires a successful network visit; cached files remain in browser-managed storage until the browser or site update removes them.

03 / CURRENT FEATURES

What is not connected today.

  • No visitor account or profile system is currently provided.
  • No payment, booking checkout or live travel inventory is connected.
  • The current public product catalogue is not operational.
  • No external AI Concierge is connected.
  • No analytics or advertising tracker was found in the homepage application source during the 26 November 2026 audit; this must be checked again before any future measurement or advertising service is added.
04 / FUTURE CHANGES

We will update this page when the product changes.

Any future account, AI, analytics, newsletter, payment, affiliate or community feature needs a fresh data-flow and privacy review before it is enabled. The production policy must identify the actual operator and a verified contact route before personal data is collected.
