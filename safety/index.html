01 / CONSENT AND CONNECTION

People are never products.

VELORA is an adult-oriented, non-explicit experience and lifestyle concept. It is not a pornography platform, escort marketplace, sexual-services platform or public stranger-matching service.

01 / CONSENT

Choice matters.

Respect boundaries, preferences and the freedom to change one's mind. A surprise, public setting or shared plan must never pressure another person into a response.

02 / PRIVACY

Ask before sharing.

Do not publish another person's image, identity, location or private conversation without their permission. Keep personal information out of the current prototype.

03 / FUTURE FEATURES

Safety before scale.

There is no public user-to-user messaging, identity verification, matching or community moderation workflow in the current product. Such features require dedicated safeguards and operations before release.

02 / TRAVEL PREPARATION

Prepare with current, local information.

Check official sources

Entry rules, local laws, health conditions, transport, weather and emergency arrangements can change. Confirm requirements for your nationality, route and travel dates with the relevant official authority and provider.

VELORA's current guides are informational and do not monitor conditions or replace local advice.

Make a practical plan

Keep essential documents secure, share an appropriate itinerary with someone you trust, confirm transport and accommodation details, and choose activities that match your needs and abilities.

Do not rely on an illustrative planner output as a verified route, booking or safety assessment.

03 / WELLBEING

Care without overclaiming.

VELORA shares general lifestyle and preparation ideas only. It is not a medical, mental-health, legal or emergency provider. Consult appropriately qualified professionals for individual advice or urgent support.
