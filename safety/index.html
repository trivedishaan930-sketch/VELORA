GOOD TO KNOW · SAFETY

Respect comes
first.

A thoughtful experience should protect comfort, choice, dignity and clear expectations.

Last reviewed: 26 November 2026

1. Adults only

VELORA is designed for adults aged 18 and over. Its romance and connection content is non-explicit. It is not a pornography, escort or sexual-services platform.

2. Consent and boundaries

Respect each person's stated boundaries. Do not pressure anyone into a date, trip, photograph, surprise or public gesture. A surprise should never remove someone's freedom to decline or change the plan. Do not create or share another person's image or personal information without appropriate permission.

3. Current product limits

The current website does not provide real-person matching, user-to-user messaging, identity verification, community moderation or emergency response. The Concierge is a local rules-based demonstration, not a staffed safety service or connected AI assistant.

4. Travel and personal safety

Check current official travel advisories, local laws, entry requirements and provider details. Choose transport and activities you can verify, share relevant plans with someone you trust where appropriate, and respect local people, communities and cultural sites.

VELORA is not an emergency service. In an emergency, contact the appropriate local emergency services or qualified professionals in your location.

5. Future safety requirements

Any future real-person connection, community, image-creation or AI feature must have its own consent, privacy, abuse-prevention, reporting, moderation and response design before it becomes available. A future label is not a claim that the service is currently operating.
