01 / WHAT VELORA IS

An inspiration and planning prototype.

VELORA provides editorial information, travel-readiness guides and browser-based planning tools for adults. The current product is not a travel agency, booking operator, airline, hotel, payment processor, medical provider or real-person matchmaking service.

Illustrative planning

The getaway planner, style helper and Concierge produce general ideas or locally generated examples. They do not verify routes, weather, entry requirements, hotel availability, supplier quality, prices, safety conditions or reservations.

The budget calculator uses figures entered by the visitor. Currency selection changes the display label; it does not perform foreign-exchange conversion or fetch live prices.

Verify before acting

Before spending money or travelling, confirm current requirements with the appropriate government authority, transport provider, accommodation, insurer or other qualified provider. Read the provider's own cancellation, refund, safety and usage terms.

Any third-party website or service is governed by its own terms and privacy information.

02 / RESPONSIBLE USE

Keep information accurate and respectful.

  • Use the prototype lawfully and respectfully.
  • Do not submit sensitive personal, identity, financial or medical information.
  • Do not upload or share another person's image or personal information without permission.
  • Do not treat illustrative suggestions as professional, emergency, medical, legal or financial advice.
  • Do not assume a provider link or destination card means VELORA has verified or endorsed that provider.
03 / AVAILABILITY AND CHANGES

Prototype features may change.

Local demonstrations, guides and tools may be changed, removed or temporarily unavailable as the product develops. Saved information is held in the visitor's browser and may be lost when browser data is cleared. No cross-device account or plan-sync service is promised.

04 / FUTURE SERVICES

Not yet a booking or commerce service.

Live catalogue, affiliate purchases, accounts, community, payments, bookings and external AI services are not represented as active capabilities unless a later release explicitly identifies and tests them.
