These terms describe the current informational and planning prototype.

Last reviewed: 26 November 2026

1. What VELORA provides today

VELORA provides inspiration, educational content, travel-readiness guides and browser-based planning demonstrations. It is not currently a travel agency, booking operator, hotel supplier, payment processor, dating or matching service, or medical provider.

2. Estimates and demonstrations

Planner outputs are illustrative starting points, not verified itineraries. The Trip Budget Planner calculates from numbers you enter; it does not fetch live fares, provider prices, exchange rates, taxes or availability. Style and Concierge features are local demonstrations and do not connect to an external AI provider.

3. Verify before making decisions

Entry rules, visas, weather, safety conditions, transport, accommodation, provider terms, prices and availability can change. Confirm important details with current official sources and the relevant provider before spending money or travelling. VELORA does not guarantee the accuracy or suitability of third-party information.

4. Respectful use

VELORA is intended for adults aged 18 and over. Use it lawfully and respectfully. Do not use the service to harass, deceive, violate another person's privacy or upload material without rights and consent.

5. Third-party services and future features

When you follow an external link, the third party's conditions and privacy policy apply. No booking, payment, public matching, direct messaging or live product catalogue is offered by this starter release. Any future commercial service will require separate terms and appropriate support, privacy and legal review.

This page is a plain-language starter notice, not legal advice. It should be reviewed by qualified counsel before commercial launch.
