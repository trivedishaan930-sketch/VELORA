# VELORA — Operating Plan & Founder Charter

**Owner / Founder:** Shubhechhu Tripathi  
**Operating support:** ChatGPT (virtual product, engineering, research and growth partner)  
**Version:** 1.0 · 24 November 2026  
**Primary business objective:** Build a useful, trusted travel-and-lifestyle product that can reach a first measurable revenue milestone of ₹1,000/month without upfront spend wherever possible.

> Revenue is a target, not a guarantee. Program approvals, audience growth, bookings and payouts are outside the website's direct control.

## 1. Brand and market position

**Brand promise:** VELORA helps people turn a travel idea into a thoughtful, well-planned experience.

**Initial acquisition wedge:** India-first couples planning honeymoons, anniversaries, romantic weekends and short escapes. The public brand remains English-language and globally presentable; early acquisition and commerce tests prioritise the Indian audience and INR-friendly decisions.

**Secondary audiences:** solo explorers and broader romance/lifestyle seekers. They remain part of the brand, but early marketing should not try to speak to everyone at once.

**Positioning line:** *Make room for romance. Discover places. Plan with confidence. Make the moment yours.*

VELORA is an inspiration and planning publisher at first—not a travel agency, booking operator, hotel supplier, dating service or medical/wellness provider. It must not imply that it can confirm live hotel inventory, prices, reservations or travel conditions unless a verified provider integration is actually in place.

## 2. Product strategy

Build around one useful visitor journey:

1. **Discover:** Searchable destination and occasion guides.
2. **Decide:** Clear comparisons—who a destination suits, seasonality, route, realistic cost assumptions, trade-offs and what to verify.
3. **Plan:** A useful trip planner that turns destination, dates, duration, travel party, interests and budget into an editable outline.
4. **Act:** Clearly labelled links to appropriate official providers or approved affiliate partners.
5. **Return:** Saveable lists, checklists, updated guides, seasonal planning and original stories.

The planner should remain free at the start. A visitor should receive a useful result before seeing a commercial recommendation. Later, test paid downloadable planning packs or an optional human-reviewed itinerary service only after users demonstrate demand and the payment, refund and support processes are ready.

## 3. Monetisation: test in this order

### A. Travel essentials — Amazon India Associates

This is the first practical affiliate application to prepare for. Amazon's published November 2026 fee schedule lists category-specific rates; for example, luggage and bags are 5.63% and luxury beauty 6.4%. Rates change and must be rechecked before publishing links.

Amazon's application review guidance says it looks for robust original content (a practical guide is at least 10 posts), recent publicly available content, and at least three qualifying sales in the first 180 days after signup. Its NEFT threshold is ₹1,000 and payment is approximately 60 days after the end of the earning month. These conditions make it a better early cash-flow test than a high-threshold overseas program, but approval and sales are not assured.

Official references:
- https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/node/topic/G8TW5AE9XL2VX9VM
- https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/node/topic/GRXPHT8U84RAYDXZ
- https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/node/topic/GKDG94FQSRXSJCGK

### B. Experiences — Viator

Viator's official partner resource centre advertises 8% commission on eligible experiences booked within a 30-day attribution window. Commission is paid after the experience is completed. Bank payout has a $50 minimum; PayPal has a different payout schedule and requirements. It is a secondary channel because the bank threshold is materially higher than the initial ₹1,000 milestone.

Official references:
- https://partnerresources.viator.com/
- https://partnerresources.viator.com/resources/commission-and-payouts/

### C. Hotels and broader travel — Trip.com (later test)

Trip.com advertises up to 7% basic commission and a 30-day website cookie, but the published bank-transfer payout minimum is US$200. Do not rely on it for the first small payout. Recheck terms, geography, traffic requirements and attribution rules at application time.

Official reference:
- https://www.trip.com/partners/index

Other hotel/activity programs may be evaluated only after their official terms, Indian publisher eligibility, attribution window, reversal rules and payout methods are verified.

### D. Future direct products

Only after a visitor audience exists:
- Low-cost printable honeymoon / weekend getaway planners.
- Destination-specific editable itinerary packs.
- Optional itinerary review service with clear scope, delivery time, cancellations and refund terms.

Do not create a paid service that promises bookings or prices VELORA cannot actually verify.

## 4. Search and content strategy

Do not invent keyword-volume figures. Search Console data becomes meaningful only after the property is verified and Google has begun showing the site. Search Console can report impressions, clicks, CTR, pages, countries, devices and queries that lead users to the site.

Initial topic hypotheses to validate:
- Honeymoon destinations in India by budget, season and trip length.
- Romantic weekend getaways in India: 2-day, 3-day and 5-day planning.
- Honeymoon packing checklist for different climates.
- Beach vs hill-station honeymoon: decision guide.
- How to plan a honeymoon budget: transport, stay, food, transfers and activities.
- Anniversary trip ideas: short break vs longer escape.
- Solo travel first-trip checklist, safety planning and accommodation considerations.
- Travel essentials: what to compare before buying luggage, bags, footwear and personal-care items.
- Destination-specific guides with route options, realistic cost assumptions and official verification links.

**Editorial standard:** each article must answer a real decision, explain its assumptions, identify what may change, include sources and show meaningful original analysis. Do not mass-publish thin AI paraphrases, fabricated hotel rankings, invented reviews, made-up prices or fake first-hand travel claims. Where no first-hand visit occurred, say so. Google's guidance prioritises helpful, reliable, people-first content and high-quality original reviews.

References:
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/write-high-quality-reviews
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/qualify-outbound-links

## 5. Social distribution

Start with a repeatable, low-cost content system:
- Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts: destination mood, trip-planning mistakes, budget breakdown frameworks, packing tips and short itinerary ideas.
- Instagram carousels: saveable checklists, comparisons, seasonal guides and couple-trip planning steps.
- Pinterest: destination boards and evergreen planning graphics, where the visual format fits.
- Website: the full, sourced guide and planner that each social post points to.

Do not buy followers, use engagement farms, spam communities or misrepresent synthetic imagery as a real customer or first-hand trip. Social posts that promote a partner for compensation must be clearly disclosed.

## 6. Measurement and operating dashboard

The first analytics stack should be free or free-tier:
- Google Search Console for queries, impressions, clicks, CTR, index coverage and country/device insights.
- Google Analytics 4 or an equivalent privacy-conscious analytics setup for visits, engaged sessions and planner interactions, with an appropriate consent/privacy approach.
- A lightweight spreadsheet for affiliate applications, outbound clicks, approved sales, reversals and payout thresholds.

Track weekly:
1. Indexed pages and Search Console impressions.
2. Organic clicks and top non-branded queries.
3. Landing-page engagement and planner starts/completions.
4. Affiliate outbound clicks.
5. Confirmed qualifying purchases / completed experiences.
6. Commission approved, pending, reversed and paid.
7. Returning visitors and saved-plan engagement where measurement is privacy-safe.

Never optimise solely for page views. The commercial funnel is useful visit → completed plan → relevant outbound click → qualifying completed transaction.

## 7. Trust, disclosure and compliance

- Put an easy-to-understand affiliate disclosure close to commercial links and recommendations.
- Use a clear disclosure label such as **Affiliate** / **Affiliate link** where applicable. ASCI says disclosures must be prominent and understandable; material connections can include gifts, discounts, trips and other benefits, not only cash.
- Mark paid/affiliate outbound links with `rel="sponsored"` as Google recommends.
- Keep privacy, terms, contact and editorial-method pages accurate.
- Do not collect email, precise location, phone, identity, payment or travel-profile data unless it is necessary, disclosed, protected and supported by an appropriate privacy and consent process.
- The Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 were notified in November 2025 with phased commencement. Before adding accounts, forms, newsletter databases or server-side profiles, check the provisions then in force and obtain Indian legal advice for production compliance.

## 8. Technology and cost policy

**Chosen production stack:** Cloudflare Pages for the frontend, Pages Functions for same-origin APIs, and D1 for the small curated product catalogue. This is the zero-cost-first production direction. GitHub Pages remains a temporary fallback only until Cloudflare is deployed and tested; then the GitHub Pages deployment should be disabled.

The current browser-only planner stays client-side. Do not add customer accounts, payment processing, stored trip profiles or a general user database until a demonstrated product need and privacy/legal design justify them. The D1 database is initially limited to curated Amazon ASINs and short-lived Amazon catalog cache. Keep secrets in Cloudflare Pages encrypted secrets, never in GitHub or browser JavaScript.

No paid ads, paid software subscriptions, custom domain, contractors, paid Cloudflare upgrade or other spend without the founder's explicit approval. The chosen free tiers have usage limits; monitor usage and only upgrade when actual demand/reliability justifies the cost. See [Cloudflare deployment and architecture](docs/CLOUDFLARE-DEPLOYMENT.md).

## 9. First 90-day execution sequence

### Foundation
- Stabilise mobile experience and test it on real devices.
- Add and verify sitemap, robots, canonical URLs, metadata, social previews and structured data where genuinely appropriate.
- Set up Search Console and analytics with the founder's account authorization.
- Complete trust pages and editorial / affiliate disclosure framework.

### Validation
- Publish at least 10 substantial original guides before applying to Amazon Associates.
- Build planner journeys that link to relevant guide pages.
- Prepare first affiliate comparison pages using current, verifiable offers and transparent disclosures.
- Launch a small social content library that points people to the guides and planner.

### Revenue test
- Apply to the most suitable affiliate programs once the site meets their requirements.
- Place tracked links only after approval.
- Compare outbound clicks with completed, approved transactions.
- Improve the topics and pages that actually produce qualified intent.
- Test one paid downloadable planner only if users show willingness to pay.

## 10. Decision rights and working protocol

**Founder retains:** ownership, legal identity, account access, bank/payment details, tax decisions, paid spending, final legal approvals and binding contracts.

**ChatGPT can execute when tools and permissions allow:** research, code changes, content drafts, page implementation, technical checks, SEO analysis, reports, campaign drafts and partner outreach drafts. It cannot guarantee income, independently hold funds, sign contracts, complete identity checks, or work in the background between messages. Every external account connection and sensitive action needs the founder's participation or explicit approval.

When the founder sends **“VELORA PUSH”**, continue from the current repository state: inspect the latest commit and metrics available, choose the highest-impact next task, implement it where safe, verify it, and report the result plus only the specific action needed from the founder.

**Operating principle:** build trust first, useful product second, distribution third, monetisation fourth. Revenue must come from real value—not misleading claims, spam or shortcuts.
