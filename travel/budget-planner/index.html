SHARE OF TOTAL

Your cost breakdown will appear here.

The result is only as accurate as the numbers entered. Check whether provider quotes include taxes, baggage, transfers, deposits, cancellation charges and other fees. A contingency reserve does not guarantee that the trip will stay within budget.

What goes into the total?

Flights, accommodation, local transport, insurance, connectivity and other costs are entered as group totals. Food and activities are entered per person and multiplied by travellers and trip duration where applicable.

What does this tool not do?

It does not find destinations, predict fares, fetch exchange rates, compare providers, calculate visa eligibility or reserve anything. Use verified provider prices and official information when you turn an estimate into a real plan.
