INDIA OUTBOUND · FIRST-TIME TRAVELLERS

Your first trip
beyond borders.

A confident first international trip is less about knowing everything—and more about checking the right things in the right order.

This practical checklist is for Indian travellers preparing to leave India. It follows core precautions published by India's Ministry of External Affairs. The destination and transit authorities—not VELORA—decide entry requirements.

THE ORDER OF DECISIONS

Check the foundations
before the excitement.

Start with documents and entry conditions. Then build your budget, insurance, accommodation and first-day plan around the trip that is actually possible.

1. Passport, visa and transit

India's Ministry of External Affairs advises travellers to ensure passport validity and currently recommends at least six months' validity from the planned date of return to India. The destination or transit country may impose different or stricter conditions, so check the exact rule for your passport and itinerary.

Confirm visa or entry permission for every country you enter or transit. A transit visa may be required even if you do not plan to leave the airport. Match the visa category to the actual purpose of travel and confirm permission is granted before departure.

2. Destination and trip fit

Choose around available time, total budget, climate, mobility, interests and practical entry conditions—not only a photograph or headline airfare.

Compare the full journey: flights, baggage, airport transfers, accommodation, local movement, meals, insurance, connectivity, attraction costs and a reserve for unexpected expenses.

03 / MONEY

Know how you will pay.

Check international usage, foreign transaction fees and limits with your bank. Keep a second permitted payment option and understand local cash needs.

04 / COVER

Check insurance and medicines.

Review travel and medical cover, exclusions and emergency contacts. Check whether medicines or ingredients are restricted at the destination or transit point.

05 / ARRIVAL

Make the first night clear.

Confirm accommodation, check-in time and airport transfer. Save the full address, booking confirmation and route option offline. Share the itinerary with someone you trust.

Keep your documents resilient.

  • Keep your passport and required documents secure.
  • Store copies of passport/visa pages, insurance and bookings separately from originals.
  • Leave an itinerary and contact method with someone at home.
  • Keep emergency contacts and insurer assistance details accessible offline.
  • Know how to contact the nearest Indian Embassy or Consulate.

Learn the rules of the place.

Read current travel advice, local laws, customs, transport guidance and restrictions for your activities. Rules around medicines, photography, driving and public behaviour can differ substantially.

For longer stays, MEA advises considering registration with the local Indian Embassy or Consulate before departure or soon after arrival. The process depends on the destination.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you leave India.

Is six months of passport validity a universal rule?

No. MEA recommends at least six months' validity from the planned date of return to India. Destination and transit authorities may use different or stricter rules. Confirm for your route and airline.

Do I need a transit visa if I stay inside the airport?

Sometimes. It depends on nationality, airport, terminal change, airline, itinerary and transit-country rules. Check with official immigration authority and airline.

Should I buy insurance even for a short trip?

Review suitable travel and medical cover for the destination, activities, trip cost and personal circumstances. Read exclusions and emergency assistance terms.

How can I contact India while overseas?

Identify the nearest Indian Embassy/Consulate before departure and save its official contact page. MEA's consular guide explains the scope and limits of assistance.
