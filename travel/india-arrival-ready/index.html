BEFORE YOU FLY

Get the essentials
in order first.

Complete official entry steps early, then make sure you can reach your stay, contact someone you trust and access what you need without a mobile signal.

Visa and arrival information are different.

The Government of India's official portal states that foreign nationals entering India generally need a valid travel document and an appropriate visa, subject to applicable exemptions and categories. e-Visa eligibility, permitted purpose, validity and authorised entry points depend on the applicant and visa category.

The official portal also provides an e-Arrival Card. Its current instructions say foreign nationals and OCI cardholders can submit arrival information online within 72 hours before arrival. The e-Arrival Card is not a visa and does not replace a required visa or other entry permission.

Use the official Government portal for the application route that matches your case. The portal warns travellers about intermediaries charging for emergency/express visa or e-Arrival services.

Make the first night simple.

  • Keep the full accommodation name and address available offline.
  • Confirm check-in time and late-arrival arrangements.
  • Plan an airport transfer using an official desk or provider you have independently verified.
  • Save the pickup point and booking details; verify the vehicle and driver.
  • Share your first-night plan with someone you trust.
YOUR FIRST 72 HOURS

Three stages.
Less last-minute stress.

Use this as a planning sequence, not a fixed schedule. Your arrival city, flight time, mobility and trip purpose should shape the details.

STAGE 01 / BEFORE DEPARTURE

Have the essentials ready.

Confirm passport and visa status, follow current e-Arrival instructions, save accommodation information, check connectivity and carry more than one suitable payment option.

STAGE 02 / AT THE AIRPORT

Follow official processes.

Use airport signs and staff instructions for immigration, baggage and arrivals. Keep documents accessible and use a transport option you can verify.

STAGE 03 / FIRST DAYS

Start at a human pace.

Rest after a long flight, check local conditions with current sources, confirm tomorrow's transport and learn local norms relevant to the places you visit.

Connectivity and payments

Check whether your phone is unlocked and supports local network bands before choosing roaming, an eSIM or a local SIM. Confirm provider rules, document requirements and coverage for your arrival location.

Keep a backup payment method. Check international usage, fees and alerts with your bank/card issuer. Do not assume a particular digital payment method will be available to every visitor or merchant.

Safety and local respect

Keep valuables and identity documents secure, use transport you can verify and be cautious with unsolicited help or payment requests. Check current local advice instead of relying on generic claims about an entire city.

Ask before photographing people, respect rules at religious and cultural sites and follow local instructions.

Emergency: India's Ministry of Home Affairs identifies 112 as the nationwide unified emergency number. The National Portal of India also lists Tourist Helpline 1363 / 1800-11-1363; check whether it is reachable and operating for your situation before relying on it.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Know the difference.

Is the e-Arrival Card a visa?

No. The official e-Arrival page explicitly says it is for arrival information, not a visa. Check whether you separately need a visa or other entry permission.

Does every visitor qualify for an Indian e-Visa?

No. Eligibility and conditions depend on nationality/region, purpose, category and authorised entry points. Confirm your case on the official portal.

Should I plan a full sightseeing day immediately after landing?

Keep the first day flexible: reach your stay, hydrate, rest, confirm local arrangements and adjust to delays or the time difference.

Can I rely on one payment or connectivity option?

Do not assume that. Check your bank and service provider, then prepare a suitable backup.

Official sources and verification

  • India e-Visa — official Government of India portal. Check eligibility, category, documents, fees and authorised entry points.
  • India e-Arrival Card — Bureau of Immigration. Arrival information, not a visa; see current submission instructions.
  • Official e-Visa instructions and FAQ. Includes application guidance and warnings about unauthorised intermediaries.
  • Ministry of Home Affairs — Emergency Response Support System (112).
  • National Portal of India — Helpline directory. Includes the tourist helpline listing; verify current reachability.
