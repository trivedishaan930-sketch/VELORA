## India inbound sources checked
1. India Visa Online, official portal: https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa/
2. India e-Arrival Card, Bureau of Immigration: https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/earrival/
3. e-Visa instructions and FAQ: https://www.indianvisaonline.gov.in/visa/tvoa.html
4. Ministry of Home Affairs, Emergency Response Support System: https://www.mha.gov.in/emergency-response-support-system-erss
5. National Portal of India, helpline directory: https://www.india.gov.in/directory/helpline

The official portal says the e-Arrival Card is for arrival information, not a visa. Current guidance describes submission by foreign nationals and OCI cardholders within 72 hours before arrival. Never imply that it grants entry or replaces a required visa or other permission. The portal also warns against unauthorised intermediaries.

MHA describes 112 as the nationwide unified emergency number. The National Portal lists Tourist Helpline 1363 / 1800-11-1363; the public guide tells readers to check current availability rather than guaranteeing service conditions.

## India outbound sources checked
1. MEA, Dos and Don'ts for Foreign Travel: https://www.mea.gov.in/dos-and-donts-for-foreign-travel.htm
2. MEA, Guide to Consular Services: https://www.mea.gov.in/guide-to-consular-services-menu.htm
3. MEA, Indian Missions Abroad: https://www.mea.gov.in/indian-mission-abroad.htm
4. Passport Seva: https://www.passportindia.gov.in/

MEA recommends passport validity of at least six months from the planned return date to India. Present this as MEA guidance, not a universal rule for every destination. Destination and transit requirements can be different or stricter.

## Product and privacy boundaries
- VELORA is an independent publisher/planning platform, not a government authority, immigration adviser, airline, insurer or travel agent.
- Browser checklists save only checkbox item IDs in local storage. They do not collect names, passport numbers, nationality, itinerary or exact location.
- No live weather, bookings, transport routes, visa eligibility API, provider quotes or availability are connected in this release.
- Never imply the guide confirms entry permission, booking, safety conditions or real-time prices.


## Trip Budget Planner — product calculation notes

Public route: `/travel/budget-planner/`. The tool performs deterministic calculations entirely in the browser. Group totals are entered for flights, accommodation, local transport, insurance, connectivity and other costs. Food is per person per day and multiplied by traveller count and trip days. Activities are per person for the trip and multiplied by traveller count. A user-selected contingency percentage is applied to the base estimate.

Currency is a display/format selection only. All amounts must be entered in the same currency; no foreign-exchange conversion, live fare, live provider price, tax estimate or booking availability is fetched. The optional budget ceiling is for the entire group. The saved draft feature stores only numeric estimates and trip counts in the current browser's local storage, only when the user chooses to save. It is not uploaded to VELORA.

The tool is a planning estimator, not a quote, booking, financial guarantee or substitute for provider fee/tax terms. Reconcile all amounts with current provider quotes before purchase.
