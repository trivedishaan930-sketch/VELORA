## 1. Executive decision

The existing North Star already defines VELORA's product, eight departments, monetisation ladder, competitor position, SEO architecture, content quality, broad social distribution and basic measurement principles. The Business Plan already defines the first travel/couple acquisition wedge, social formats, Search Console, analytics, affiliate tracking, disclosure and a phased operating sequence.

The remaining gap was an integrated marketing operating system: audience-to-channel priorities, funnel definitions, campaign design, editorial distribution, lifecycle communication, creator/partner operations, conversion optimisation, attribution, experiment governance, budget gates and launch checklists.

This playbook closes that gap. It does not replace the North Star, promise revenue or authorise spending.

### Marketing objective

Acquire the right people, help them complete a useful experience plan, earn trust, and make a relevant next step possible. Marketing reinforces the VELORA Moment-to-Memory Loop:

**Discover → Personalise → Design → Prepare → Act → Remember / Return**

- **Product North Star:** Monthly Completed Experience Plans.
- **Marketing's job:** bring qualified users into a useful journey and reduce friction until they complete, save, share or act on a plan.
- **Commercial path:** qualified discovery → useful landing page → planner/guide engagement → completed plan → relevant disclosed partner click or paid product → approved transaction/contribution → repeat use or referral.

Page views, follower counts and impressions are diagnostic metrics, not business outcomes by themselves.

**Operating principle:** Build trust first, useful product second, distribution third, monetisation fourth. Do not buy traffic to an unfinished promise.

## 2. Evidence labels and research limits

- **Verified external requirement / platform guidance:** grounded in a linked official source. Policies can change; re-check before execution.
- **Observed project evidence:** an audit or research result already recorded in the VELORA repository/OpenSEO snapshot.
- **VELORA recommendation:** a strategic decision based on the product vision and available evidence; not a platform rule or guaranteed result.
- **Hypothesis to test:** insufficient VELORA first-party data exists to call it proven.

### Known and unknown

The 25 November 2026 OpenSEO research was a directional sample, not a comprehensive market-size or competitor-financial study. It showed strong competition from established booking marketplaces, tour operators and travel publishers on broad travel terms. Example estimated India monthly search volumes included “weekend getaways from Delhi” (6,600), “honeymoon packages in India” (4,400) and “Bali trip cost from India” (4,400). The exact seed “romantic weekend getaways in India” did not return a reliable volume in that research.

The prior project audit found a one-page/hash-navigation foundation, no connected Search Console property, and no reliable first-party conversion or commission dataset. OpenSEO's crawl captured one indexable page and no crawlable outgoing links.

Therefore:
- Do not claim market share, total addressable market, CAC, LTV, conversion benchmarks or expected earnings without new evidence.
- Do not use keyword estimates as a traffic forecast.
- Do not call a channel or content format a proven winner until VELORA tests it.
- First establish a measurable baseline; do not manufacture impressive growth numbers.

## 4. Positioning, message and CTA system

### Positioning

VELORA is an experience and lifestyle planning ecosystem that helps adults discover, design, prepare for and enjoy meaningful moments. It is not an inventory-first OTA, travel agency, generic dating platform, social network, escort service or generic AI chatbot.

### Message hierarchy

1. Brand: **Make Room for Romance.**
2. Promise: **Make meaningful experiences easier to imagine, plan and enjoy.**
3. Functional proof: guides, planners, practical checklists, curated ideas, source-linked information and relevant next steps.
4. Emotional result: more confidence, less planning friction and a moment that feels personal.
5. Commercial clarity: affiliate, sponsored and paid recommendations are labelled; VELORA does not claim to operate a booking or service that it does not provide.

### Draft message directions

- Couples: “Plan a trip that feels like yours — from destination choice to the details.”
- Outbound: “Choose with confidence. Compare season, budget and practical readiness before you book.”
- India inbound: “Arrive prepared. Understand your first 72 hours in India before you land.”
- Experiences: “Turn an occasion into a plan, not another open browser tab.”
- Style: “Build a practical travel wardrobe around the place, weather and moment.”

### CTA by funnel stage

- Discovery: Explore the guide / See destination options.
- Consideration: Compare options / Build your plan.
- Planning: Personalise this plan / Get the checklist.
- Completion: Save / Export / Share your plan.
- Commercial: Check the provider's current details, only where real, relevant and verified.
- Return: Continue your plan / Explore the next occasion.

Avoid fake urgency, false scarcity, guaranteed savings, invented reviews, unsupported “best” claims, misleading provider affiliation or implied live availability that is not integrated.

## 5. Channel strategy and order of investment

The sequence below is a VELORA recommendation, not a universal ranking. Reassess with first-party evidence.

### Tier 1 — Owned foundations

**Website and tools**
- Each campaign should lead to a relevant, crawlable page or useful planner—not a generic homepage by default.
- Each key URL needs one clear user/search intent, one primary action and meaningful internal links.
- Mobile speed, legibility, accessibility, trust and honest feature status are conversion requirements.
- Do not collect details simply because a form or analytics product makes it possible.

**Search / SEO**
- Build durable pages around high-intent decisions: destination cost framework, season choice, itinerary trade-offs, first-time arrival readiness, official visa/entry preparation, honeymoon and anniversary planning, packing and experience design.
- One strong page per coherent intent; merge overlapping topics. Avoid near-identical pages for each keyword, city, month or destination.
- Put direct answers and practical summary near the top, then explain assumptions, alternatives, sources and update dates.
- Add original comparisons, calculations, tools, checklists or research. Do not merely rewrite competitors.
- Keep titles, descriptions, headings, internal links, canonical URLs, sitemap and structured data accurate.
- Use Search Console for indexed pages, queries, countries, devices, clicks, impressions and CTR once connected.
- AI-assisted content requires human fact-checking and meaningful value; it is not a publishing-volume shortcut.

**Trust pages**
About, Contact, Editorial / Research Method, Affiliate Disclosure, Privacy, Terms and Safety must be accurate and easy to find before meaningful commercial promotion.

### Tier 2 — Organic discovery

**Instagram**
- Role: visual discovery and brand familiarity, especially destinations, couple experiences, style and short planning tips.
- Formats: short vertical video, saveable checklists/comparison carousels, stories and occasional research behind-the-scenes.
- Each post should have one idea and one next step.
- Never present AI imagery as customer photography, a real trip or first-hand evidence.

**YouTube**
- Role: deeper searchable explanations and trust.
- Formats: destination-choice walkthroughs, budget assumptions, India arrival preparation, travel mistakes, itinerary trade-offs, packing and occasion planning.
- Link to a full source guide. Do not imply on-location experience unless it happened.

**Pinterest**
- Role: evergreen visual search and saving: travel planning, style, wedding/honeymoon inspiration, checklists and occasion boards.
- Use intent-specific boards and direct links to useful pages.
- Research regional trends before seasonal creative. Pinterest's official Trends tool describes historical search/save/shopping trend data by selected region; feature availability varies.
- Avoid mass-produced near-duplicate pins and misleading destinations.

**Relevant communities**
- Participate where real questions are discussed: travel, India travel, destinations, couples, wedding planning, style and solo travel.
- Follow community rules. Be helpful, disclose affiliation and link only when relevant and permitted.
- No unsolicited DMs, repeated link drops, fake accounts, engagement pods or disguised promotions.

### Tier 3 — Permission-based retention

**Email / newsletter**
- Do not collect email merely to build a list. Offer a real reason: destination-readiness updates, planning templates, seasonal guides or a useful digest.
- Use affirmative opt-in, clear frequency expectations, a privacy notice and working unsubscribe.
- Keep marketing consent distinct from essential service messages. Do not use purchased, scraped or borrowed lists.
- Start with a small welcome sequence: what VELORA is, the promised resource, preference/unsubscribe controls and one relevant next guide.
- Lifecycle messages (saved-plan reminder, destination update, planner help, new guide) require a real feature and an appropriate consent basis.
- Do not email users about sensitive relationship, sexual-health or precise travel details inferred from behaviour.

### Tier 4 — Borrowed trust and partnerships

**Creator / influencer partnerships**
- Start with small, relevant creator pilots whose audience overlaps one P1 use case.
- Evaluate geography, content quality, comment authenticity, trust, brand safety, disclosure and landing-page fit—not follower count alone.
- Each collaboration needs a brief, tracked URL, disclosure instructions, deliverables, rights, timeline and reporting window.
- Prefer co-created guides, checklists, destination explainers and planner walkthroughs over generic shout-outs.
- Never pay for fake reviews, guaranteed rankings, fake engagement or unsupported claims.
- In India, check current ASCI guidance. Material connections can include money, gifts, discounts, trips, stays or other benefits; disclosures must be prominent and understandable for the format.
- For international campaigns, check local endorsement and consumer-protection rules; review FTC endorsement guidance for US-facing work where applicable.

**Supplier and destination partnerships**
- Potential categories: stays, activities, transfers, transport, eSIM, insurance, local experts, tourism organisations, fashion and lifestyle brands.
- Establish what VELORA brings: audience relevance, qualified referral, useful content, transparent placement and reporting.
- Confirm commercial terms, markets, approved claims, cancellation/commission rules, attribution, deep links, tracking, creative rights, data sharing and payment terms in writing.
- Paid placement must not silently affect editorial ranking or safety conclusions.
- Do not call a supplier a partner until an agreement exists.

**PR / earned distribution**
- Pitch a useful story, research finding, tool or expert source—not an empty announcement.
- Possible angles: First 72 Hours in India, practical planning research, responsible destination planning and occasion-led travel design.
- Claims must be reproducible and methods disclosed. Never invent survey results or expert credentials.

### Tier 5 — Paid acquisition (gated)

Do not begin with broad paid social or display.

Paid tests may be considered only after the landing page is complete, truthful, fast and usable; a real tool/guide exists; conversion events and UTMs are tested; privacy/consent and policy reviews exist; economics and stop-loss are defined; and the founder approves maximum spend.

Test one campaign, one audience/use case, one landing page and a small set of creative variants. Separate search intent from social discovery. Stop quickly when traffic is irrelevant, tracking fails, the offer is unclear or the economics cannot plausibly work.

**Policy caution:** Google treats sexual content as a sensitive interest category for personalised advertising and restricts targeting. Its sensitive-category list also includes relationship hardships and health. Meta reviews ads against its advertising/community standards, including privacy/personal-attribute and health/wellness requirements. VELORA's tasteful 18+ positioning does not guarantee approval for every romance, relationship or wellness ad. Review exact copy, creative, targeting, landing page and current policies. Never evade review by disguising content.

## 6. VELORA content engine

### One research asset, multiple useful formats

Start each campaign with one evidence-backed anchor asset:
- destination / arrival guide;
- decision comparison;
- planner / checklist;
- occasion or experience guide;
- wardrobe / packing guide;
- original activity, story or library resource.

Repurpose appropriately:
- Search: complete guide and supporting pages.
- YouTube: decision walkthrough or explainer.
- Instagram: short clips, carousel and stories.
- Pinterest: evergreen visual pins linking to the guide.
- Email: concise opt-in digest or requested update.
- Community: direct answer to a real question, link only when welcome.

Repurposing must preserve context and facts. A social summary is not the complete guide.

### Required editorial brief

Record: audience and intent; reader question; decision to solve; page/tool and CTA; evidence and source links; checked date; volatile facts; labelled estimates; author/reviewer; affiliate/sponsor connection; image rights and synthetic/illustrative status where relevant; distribution channels; tracked links; update trigger and review date.

### Quality gates

- Useful even if no commercial link is clicked.
- Original analysis, decision support or practical detail.
- No fake first-hand travel, staff expertise, customer stories, bookings or availability.
- No unsupported rankings or “best” claims.
- Current sources for volatile facts; official sources for visa, entry, safety, schedules and regulations.
- Separate verified fact, estimate, editorial judgement and illustration.
- Wellness content is general education, not diagnosis/treatment.
- Original, licensed or public-domain stories and media only.
- No doorway pages or near-identical AI-generated page farms.

### Sustainable starting cadence (hypothesis, not quota)

For a founder-led early stage:
- One substantial anchor guide or tool improvement every 1–2 weeks.
- Three to five useful derivatives across only the relevant channels.
- One update pass on an existing high-value page.
- One user-question/community research session.
- One measurement review.

Reduce cadence if verification or quality suffers. Increase only when the workflow and demand justify it.

## 8. Conversion funnel and product-led growth

| Stage | User action | Measurement | Improvement focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reach | Sees search, pin, video, post or referral | Impressions / reach | Relevance and message |
| Qualified visit | Visits relevant page | Sessions by source, intent and market | Promise-to-page match |
| Engage | Reads, compares or starts planner | Engaged sessions, planner starts | Utility and clear CTA |
| Activate | Completes useful plan | Completed plans | Planner quality |
| Save / share | Saves, exports or shares | Save/export/share events | Portable useful output |
| Act | Clicks partner or buys digital product | Qualified click / paid purchase | Trust and relevance |
| Earn | Partner approves and pays transaction | Approved, reversed, paid commission | Partner fit and quality |
| Return / refer | Comes back or shares | Repeat plans, direct/referral | Ongoing value |

**Activation definition:** a completed plan that produces a useful output or next step. Opening the planner alone is not activation.

### Growth loops to test

1. **Shareable plan:** complete plan → share/export → recipient opens a privacy-safe view → recipient starts a plan.
2. **Guide to planner:** guide answers a question → planner personalises it → plan links to related guides.
3. **Seasonal update:** opted-in user receives requested useful update → revisits and adjusts plan.
4. **Creator to tool:** creator demonstrates real tool → audience uses dedicated page → VELORA measures completed plans.
5. **Library to experience:** activity/story inspires date or trip plan → user saves or shares it.

Do not add referral rewards, public profiles or social feeds before privacy, abuse prevention, attribution and support are ready.

## 9. Analytics, attribution and data discipline

### Minimum measurement foundation

- Verify production host, canonical URLs and crawlability.
- Connect Search Console to founder-controlled property.
- Choose privacy-appropriate analytics and document consent decisions.
- Test events in a controlled environment.
- Establish UTM naming and a campaign register.
- Keep affiliate partner reporting separate; only partner reporting can confirm approved transactions and payouts.

### Proposed event taxonomy

These are proposed names, not currently implemented events. Map to GA4 recommended events where appropriate; use custom events only where needed.

| Event | Trigger | Safe example parameters |
|---|---|---|
| view_guide | Meaningful guide viewed | content_id, content_type, market |
| planner_start | Planner started | planner_type, market |
| planner_complete | Useful plan completed | planner_type, market, plan_type |
| plan_save | Save/export action | save_method |
| plan_share | Share/export initiated | share_method |
| affiliate_click | Labelled partner link clicked | partner_id, category, placement_id, content_id |
| lead_opt_in | Affirmative subscription | form_id, consent_version |
| digital_product_purchase | Secure payment confirmed | product_id, currency, value, only after real payment integration |

Do not send email, phone, name, precise location, identity, relationship/sexual-health information or free text to analytics. Never put personal data in URLs or UTMs.

### UTM convention

Use lowercase and stable values:
- utm_source: platform or partner, e.g. instagram, pinterest or creatorname.
- utm_medium: organic_social, paid_social, email, creator, referral or cpc.
- utm_campaign: audience_theme_market_period, e.g. couples_honeymoon_india_2027q1.
- utm_content: creative/placement variant, e.g. carousel_budget_checklist_a.
- utm_id: stable campaign ID when useful.

Keep an internal register mapping names to pages and partner IDs. Google Analytics campaign values are case-sensitive; avoid multiple spellings for one channel.

### Weekly dashboard

**Acquisition:** Search Console impressions, clicks, CTR and position by page/query/market; qualified sessions by source/medium/campaign; new vs returning (with privacy limitations); social/video saves and retention as supporting signals.

**Activation:** planner starts/completions; completion rate = completed plans ÷ planner starts; save/export/share rate = users doing one action ÷ completed plans; landing-to-completion by audience/channel.

**Commercial:** affiliate clicks by page/category/partner/placement; partner-confirmed conversion data; approved/pending/reversed/paid commissions; digital conversion/refunds/net receipts when payment exists; revenue per qualified session with denominator defined.

**Trust and quality:** broken links, stale facts, complaints, opt-outs, unsubscribes, spam reports, corrections, source-review age, safety incidents and mobile errors.

### Attribution rules

- An affiliate click is not a sale; a pending transaction is not approved commission.
- Do not assume all direct traffic came from the last campaign.
- Compare platform analytics, UTM data and partner reports; explain discrepancies.
- First-touch and last-non-direct are diagnostic views, not perfect truth.
- Respect consent and platform limitations. No fingerprinting or hidden tracking.

## 10. Conversion optimisation and experiments

Test one clear user problem at a time.

Areas: headline clarity; guide CTA vs planner CTA; planner length/fields; output and save friction; mobile navigation/speed; comparison tables; affiliate disclosure clarity; partner CTA relevance; opt-in value/frequency; creator landing continuity.

Every experiment records:
1. Observation from real behaviour or feedback.
2. Hypothesis: if X changes for audience Y, metric Z should improve because...
3. One main change, not five simultaneous changes.
4. Primary metric.
5. Quality guardrail (complaints, refunds, safety, opt-outs or bounce).
6. Minimum evidence; do not claim statistical certainty from tiny samples.
7. Keep, revise or revert decision.
8. Learning and next question.

Prioritise user impact, confidence, effort and risk. A planner that fails to produce a useful plan is more important than a decorative button colour.

## 11. Affiliate, creator and partner operations

### Partner qualification scorecard

Check audience/category fit; market coverage; commission base and exclusions; attribution window; reversals; payout threshold and schedule; currency/tax requirements; deep links; API/feed/image/cache permissions; price freshness; reputation/support/refunds; claims restrictions; disclosure/programme rules; and concentration risk.

Do not choose only by headline commission rate.

### Partner CRM fields

Company/programme, contact route, geography, category, fit rationale, application date/status, terms version/date, tracking link, payout threshold, reporting method, last review, issue and next action. Never put secrets or credentials in a public repository or unsecured sheet.

### Creator brief

Audience and objective; claims and prohibited claims; deliverables/deadlines/review; disclosure format; tracked URL/landing page; content usage rights and duration/territory; paid amplification permission; synthetic/real media representation; payment/cancellation; reporting; brand safety and escalation.

### Editorial independence

Payments, free stays, gifts and creator relationships must not silently change independent advice, comparison, safety assessment or ranking. Label material connections and preserve the editorial basis.

## 12. Paid media economics and budget gates

The founder approves every expense. This document authorises no paid software, advertising, contractor, domain or upgrade.

Before spend, estimate qualified-session cost, landing-to-plan completion, completed-plan-to-partner-click, partner click-to-approved transaction, effective commission after reversals/fees, and contribution after variable costs. For digital goods include fees, taxes, refunds, support and fulfilment. For membership include recurring cost, churn and support.

Formulas:

- Qualified sessions = impressions × click-through rate × qualified-visit share.
- Completed plans = qualified sessions × plan-completion rate.
- Affiliate gross commission = qualified partner clicks × approved conversion rate × eligible transaction value × effective commission rate.
- Campaign contribution = attributable net revenue − media spend − variable operating costs.
- Break-even CAC = expected contribution per acquired customer over the measured payback window.

These need real partner and customer data; they are not forecasts.

Paid test: one use case, one landing page, small approved budget, hard stop-loss, search-term/geo/device quality review. Optimise for meaningful actions, not cheap clicks. Pause on misleading expectations, policy/privacy risk or negative contribution.

## 13. Privacy, disclosure, safety and platform policy

### Data and consent
- Collect the least data needed for a clearly explained purpose.
- Use affirmative, specific, recorded marketing opt-in and easy unsubscribe.
- Separate marketing consent from essential service messages.
- No purchased/scraped lists or unsolicited bulk messaging.
- Avoid sensitive relationship, sexual-health and precise-location targeting/profiling.
- Do not upload customer lists or use remarketing without verifying legal basis, consent, platform policy and current processing setup.
- Document access, retention, deletion, vendor and breach processes before account/profile data collection.
- India's DPDP commencement is phased. Gazette G.S.R. 843(E), dated 13 November 2025, specifies some provisions from publication, certain provisions one year later, and the principal set of provisions eighteen months later (13 May 2027). Do not say the whole Act took effect at once or assume future commencement means no preparation is needed. Get current Indian legal advice before production data processing; consult the Gazette for exact sections and dates.
- For other countries, identify applicable privacy and marketing law before data collection or profiling.

### Commercial and influencer disclosure
- Put affiliate disclosure close to the recommendation/link, not only in the footer.
- Clearly label paid, sponsored, gifted and affiliate relationships.
- For India, check current ASCI guidance and applicable consumer-protection rules; disclosure must be prominent, understandable and appropriate to format.
- For US-facing endorsements, review FTC Endorsement Guides and applicable rules.
- Never buy/publish fake reviews, suppress legitimate negative feedback or incentivise undisclosed positive reviews.

### Ad and content policy
- Review current Google Ads and Meta policies before each paid campaign, especially romance, relationship, sexual-health, wellness and personalised targeting.
- Do not assert private personal attributes or exploit relationship insecurity.
- Never imply VELORA knows a user's intimate life, health status, identity or exact location.
- Keep romantic imagery tasteful and non-explicit; follow current platform rules.
- No cloaking, misleading redirects or disguised landing pages.
- Landing page must match the ad promise, state what VELORA actually provides and be easy to navigate.

### Safety and brand integrity
- 18+ positioning is not identity verification for any future feature that requires it.
- No public stranger-matching/companionship marketplace before identity, fraud, moderation, reporting, escalation, privacy and legal operations exist.
- No non-consensual intimate imagery, impersonation or misleading synthetic testimonials.
- No diagnosis, treatment promises or unsupported health claims.
- Maintain complaint, correction, takedown and partner escalation processes as operations grow.

## 14. International marketing and localisation

International expansion is a series of separate market decisions, not one global campaign.

Before entering a market, validate audience use case and search demand; local language/terminology; source availability and update burden; competitor/publisher landscape; affiliate eligibility and payout; prices/currency/tax/payment; advertising/endorsement/privacy/consumer rules; support hours; local creator/partner quality; and the ability to maintain content.

Localise only where there is real value and capacity. Use hreflang only for genuinely distinct language/region pages. Do not auto-translate and publish large page sets without expert review.

## 16. Stage-gated execution plan

These are indicative windows, not promises. Advance on exit criteria, not the calendar.

### Gate 0 — Instrument and stabilise
- Resolve verified production host, deployment and crawlability.
- Connect Search Console and privacy-appropriate analytics with founder authorisation.
- Establish and test events and UTM conventions.
- Publish accurate trust, editorial and affiliate disclosure pages.
- No paid acquisition.

**Exit:** discovery, planner completion, affiliate click and approved commission can be distinguished without exposing secrets or personal data.

### Gate 1 — Prove one useful audience journey
- Launch a small number of substantial P1 guides and one useful planner journey.
- Cover India outbound and inbound India in a maintainable way.
- Build India Arrival Ready / First 72 Hours as a real helpful experience before promoting it.
- Publish a small organic content library linked to relevant guides.
- Gather structured user feedback without manufacturing testimonials.

**Exit:** users can complete a useful plan; tracking and feedback expose success and failure points.

### Gate 2 — Prove a channel and commercial path
- Identify source(s) bringing qualified users.
- Apply to suitable partners when content, traffic and programme rules permit.
- Add approved affiliate links with disclosure and tracking.
- Compare clicks with partner-confirmed approved transactions.
- Test one small digital product only after demand and delivery capability are clear.

**Exit:** repeatable qualified demand and a legitimate commercial path—not one isolated sale or a large click count.

### Gate 3 — Build repeatable growth
- Improve winning landing pages/content clusters.
- Establish a consistent creator/partner pipeline.
- Add permission-based email only with clear user benefit.
- Test shareable plans/referrals after privacy and abuse controls exist.
- Consider paid test only with working funnel, approved budget and stop-loss.

**Exit:** acquisition and contribution measured, quality sustained and at least one channel repeatable.

### Gate 4 — Extend departments and markets
- Connect Romance & Experiences and Fashion & Style to the proven planning loop.
- Add Romance Library and responsible wellness only with editorial capacity.
- Gate Connection & Companionship and AI Creative Studio behind safety/privacy controls.
- Build real AI Concierge only with source data, evaluation, support and cost controls.
- Enter countries one at a time.

**Exit:** new department/market adds measurable value without weakening trust, quality or economics.

## 17. Stop-doing list

- No empty landing pages for all eight departments.
- No purchased followers, likes, comments, reviews or email lists.
- No mass generic AI content or near-identical destination pages.
- No spam in communities, creator inboxes or email.
- No broad paid ads before tracking and landing-page validation.
- No sensitive personal targeting or relationship-insecurity exploitation.
- No treating views, clicks, downloads or pending bookings as revenue.
- No partner promotion without terms and disclosure.
- No fabricated first-hand, customer, expert or availability claims.
- No promise of ranking, guaranteed income, booking, visa outcome or relationship result.
- No silent influence of editorial truth by commission.
- No collecting personal data “for future use”.

## 18. Monthly scorecard

Do not set arbitrary targets until baseline data exists.

| Dimension | Metric | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Product value | Monthly Completed Experience Plans | Is VELORA helping users finish something useful? |
| Acquisition | Qualified organic clicks by audience/page | Which demand is reaching us? |
| Discovery quality | Landing-to-planner-start | Does page match intent? |
| Activation | Planner completion rate | Can users make a useful plan? |
| Utility | Save/export/share rate | Is output worth keeping/sharing? |
| Commercial | Qualified affiliate click-through | Are next steps relevant and trusted? |
| Revenue | Approved/paid commission and net digital sales | Is real money being earned? |
| Efficiency | Contribution per qualified session / CAC | Is growth sustainable? |
| Retention | Repeat plans and direct/referral return | Does VELORA deserve repeat use? |
| Brand | Branded search, direct visits, referrals | Is recognition growing? |
| Quality | Corrections, broken links, complaints, opt-outs | Is growth damaging trust? |
| Safety | Incidents, reports, privacy requests, response time | Can VELORA operate responsibly? |

A channel wins when it brings the right people who complete useful plans and, where relevant, create sustainable disclosed revenue without unacceptable risk—not simply because reach is high.

## 20. Final operating rule

Before approving a channel, campaign, page, partnership or marketing tool, answer:
1. Which real user problem does it solve?
2. Which VELORA audience is it for?
3. Is the promised experience actually available and tested?
4. What evidence says this audience uses this channel for this decision?
5. How will a completed useful plan and its next step be measured?
6. What data, consent, platform-policy and disclosure risks exist?
7. What does it cost, who approves it, and when do we stop?
8. What did we learn, and where is it recorded?

If an idea fails these tests, do not ship it merely because it is fashionable.

**North Star reminder:** VELORA makes meaningful experiences easier to imagine, plan and enjoy. Marketing should make that promise easier to discover—and never bigger than the product can honestly deliver.
